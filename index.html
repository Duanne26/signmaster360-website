Frequently asked questions

Good questions. Clear answers.

Who is SignMaster 360 for?

Signage and print businesses that want to manage jobs, customers, materials, payments and team workflows in one place.

How much does it cost?

R399 per business per month, with all application features included. A 7-day free trial is planned for launch.

Can my employees use it too?

Yes. The application supports team members with different roles and permissions.

Can I use SignMaster 360 on a PC?

Yes. SignMaster 360 is a web application, so you can access it through a supported desktop browser.

Will there be an Android app?

An Android version is planned for Google Play. Availability will be announced after testing and publication.

Is my business information shared with other subscribers?

SignMaster 360 is designed to keep each subscribing business's information separate. Security and tenant-isolation checks are part of the pre-launch testing process.
