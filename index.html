    Featured automation · Stock control

    Materials used? Stock is automatically deducted.

    When a job is approved, its materials can be reserved. When production moves to Printing, the recorded material usage is deducted from the stock list automatically — with safeguards against duplicate deductions and stock movement tracking.

    📋 Job approved→📦 Materials reserved→🖨️ Printing starts→✅ Stock updated

    Material usage can be calculated using width × height × quantity, with adjustments for cancelled or deleted jobs according to the configured workflow.

    📐 Automatic material calculations

    Calculate material requirements from job dimensions and quantities.

    ⚠️ Low-stock visibility

    Spot materials running low or out of stock without manually checking every item.

    🔔 Job deadline reminders

    Set job reminders and receive alerts for approaching dates.

    🔄 Quote-to-invoice conversion

    Convert documents without entering all the customer and job information again.

    🏭 Connected production tracking

    Keep sales and production job status aligned, with completed work separated from active jobs.

    🧾 Stock movement records

    Follow reservations, usage deductions and corrections for better accountability.

    Feature behaviour depends on configuration and the version deployed. Verify automated workflows in your business workspace before relying on them for inventory decisions.

    Frequently asked questions

    Good questions. Clear answers.

    Who is SignMaster 360 for?

    Signage and print businesses that want to manage jobs, customers, materials, payments and team workflows in one place.

    How much does it cost?

    R399 per business per month, with all application features included. A 7-day free trial is planned for launch.

    Can my employees use it too?

    Yes. The application supports team members with different roles and permissions.

    Can I use SignMaster 360 on a PC?

    Yes. SignMaster 360 is a web application, so you can access it through a supported desktop browser.

    Will there be an Android app?

    An Android version is planned for Google Play. Availability will be announced after testing and publication.

    Is my business information shared with other subscribers?

    SignMaster 360 is designed to keep each subscribing business's information separate. Security and tenant-isolation checks are part of the pre-launch testing process.
